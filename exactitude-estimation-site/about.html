Our story

Precision as a discipline, not a slogan

We started Exactitude Estimation after seeing too many good projects lose money to estimates that were rushed, vague or simply wrong. Our team of estimators and quantity surveyors treats every takeoff like an engineering problem — measured, checked and documented.

Today we support residential builders, commercial developers, architects and public agencies across the USA and Canada, with more than 1,200 projects estimated and $2.4B+ in construction value covered.
