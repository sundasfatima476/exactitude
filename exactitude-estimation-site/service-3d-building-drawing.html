What's included in 3D Building Drawing

Seeing a project in three dimensions catches conflicts and quantities that flat plans miss. Our models double as a visual reference for clients and a precise source for takeoffs.

  • Full 3D building models from 2D plans
  • Clash detection between systems
  • Interior and exterior renderings
  • Quantity extraction directly from the model
  • Shareable walkthroughs for client sign-off
